# Committee Operator Guide (Non-technical)

> **v3.3 · Documentation reviewed 17 October 2026.** Current source and selected hosted schema, policies, settings and buckets were checked. This is not a full security audit, legal approval or device-delivery certification.


## Start with the right account

Use the confirmed app address and your authorised admin account. Open your account menu (mobile top-left avatar) and **Admin Moderation**. If a task below is unavailable or fails, request the developer; **never run SQL**, share a password or export member contact data to a personal device.

This guide describes controls present in source. It does not certify every action has been tested with your hosted admin session. Current supplied project address is https://gbaug.lovable.app; do not assume historical custom-domain links are attached.

## 1. The actual moderation tabs

**Access · Companies · Products · Users · Circulars · Ads · Categories · Community · Anon Log · RFQs.** No red-dot queue, refund queue or complete Approve/Reject/evidence workflow is promised by this guide.

## 2. Business review, visibility and verification

Business applications at /apply create hidden pending company records. Current Companies rows show name/city, state badges and **Verify/Unverify**, **Hide/Unhide** controls. They do not expose the full approval/rejection form described by older guides.

1. Review business identity through your approved office process.
2. Ask the developer/staff owner to resolve pending approval where the required control is absent; unhiding alone does not change a pending review status to approved.
3. Use Verify only after evidence review; it is not a paid trust badge or guarantee of quality/credit.
4. Use Hide for a visibility intervention; report suspected fraud to the officer.

Do not invent a rejection email, automatic notification, evidence attachment viewer or one-click resubmission feature. Business verification, Association membership and platform roles are distinct.

## 3. Users and roles

Users tab shows role badges and admin/paid_member/broker buttons. Change privileges only with recorded committee authorisation, especially admin. Business team roles are managed separately. A demo role selector never grants server access. Do not casually demote an owner or assign admin to solve a normal member error.

## 4. Access switches

Open **Access**:

- **Open all features to everyone** controls the feature-access setting.
- **Verification not required** controls the verification override setting.

Both were ON in the 17 October 2026 review. Guests still need signup to interact. **Known limitation:** RFQ creation/quotation rules still directly require an approved visible verified business; RFQ reads require login; contact helper still checks Paid/Broker/Admin. If a member is blocked with switches on, send the action/error to the developer rather than promising all restrictions are gone.

## 5. Publish a Bulletin

The admin tab is still labelled **Circulars**, while readers see **Bulletin** inside Home.

1. Fill Title, Body and Category in **Compose New Circular**.
2. Optionally choose up to five PDF/JPG/PNG/WebP attachments. PDFs up to 25MB each; images up to 10MB, per client validation.
3. Press **Publish**.
4. Check the Home Bulletin chip as an ordinary reader; confirm the correct text and attachment access.
5. Existing rows offer Publish/Unpublish and Delete. No rich-text toolbar or circular detail route is promised.

Attachments use private short-lived links. A guest may see text but not open attachments; do not make attachment storage public to fix that.

## 6. Run an ad

Go to **Ads → Upload New Ad**. Provide title, click-through URL, placement, priority, primary image and optional mobile image, with the available active/date controls.

Recommended creative: **1456×180 desktop, 720×200 mobile**, JPG/PNG/WebP, preferably under 300KB. These are recommendations, not hard server caps. Other ratios fit the same compact slot with letterboxing/backdrop; no bottom title strip is shown.

Placements currently offered include Homepage Banner, Search bar banner, Categories above, Directory Banner, Products Banner and Circulars Banner. Not every offered placement necessarily has a current visible slot; verify the destination before selling it. Date filtering is based on stored dates; do not promise exact local-midnight timing without checking.

## 7. Content, products, categories and RFQs

- **Community:** current feed moderation (visibility/pinning/lane actions as provided), not the retired Discourse forum.
- **Anon Log:** authorised compliance review only; never forward identities to ordinary members.
- **Products:** view, feature/unfeature, hide/unhide and delete controls. Keep range-price/stock-band rules.
- **Categories:** current category editing; featured order affects discovery.
- **RFQs:** Hide/Unhide and **Expire** (confirmation required), not a read-only monthly analytics report. Private quotations/chat stay participant-controlled.

Record serious moderation reasons through the approved office process. Use /messages for permitted business conversations; do not use an admin role as an excuse to disclose private trade content.

## 8. Notifications and installation

Members open the bell/history for activity and profile settings for notification preferences. Enable Push requires browser/device permission. Ask them to install using Get App; iOS uses Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. A history badge does not prove device sound; OS/browser settings also matter. Send unresolved delivery issues to the developer with action/time/device, not private keys or credentials.

## 9. Daily checklist

- Check pending business issues and escalate where the UI lacks an approval action.
- Confirm published Bulletin text/attachment permissions and ad destination/date/creative.
- Resolve inappropriate content under the approved moderation process.
- Keep exact commodity prices/stock and personal contact exports out of notices.
- Never circulate an anonymous identity or confidential quotation/chat outside authorised review.
- Record grievances/refunds/deletion requests with the officer, not a spreadsheet on a personal device.
