# Business & Scope

> **v3.3 · Documentation reviewed 17 October 2026.** Current source and selected hosted schema, policies, settings and buckets were checked. This is not a full security audit, legal approval or device-delivery certification.

## Purpose and boundaries

G-BAU-G is operated by MDDMA and connects legitimate food-trade businesses with identity review, controlled-transparency discovery, community activity and private commercial communication. Network verification, Association affiliation and commercial pricing are distinct.

## Current account/business proposition

| Surface | What it does now |
|---|---|
| `/membership` | Explains individual account versus verified business; not a priced subscription checkout |
| `/apply` | Registers an existing Indian/overseas business; submits a hidden pending company for staff review |
| Business approval | Evidence-based verification/visibility; not a purchased badge |
| Commercial actions | RFQ/quote/chat participation according to actual business/session policies |
| Legacy price helpers | Retain Free / ₹10,000/year Paid labels; broker same fee, no addon |
| Payments | In-app membership management disabled; hosted membership table absent |

This refresh does not change the BIZ-002/003 baseline or introduce new fees. Future commercial packaging needs an explicit committee decision. Do not promise an operational checkout, annual expiry automation or founding price lock from old payment code.

## Access override limitations

Admin → Moderation → **Access** exposes “Open all features to everyone” and “Verification not required.” Both were ON in the hosted snapshot on 17 October 2026. Guests still need signup for interactions. RFQ creation/quotations retain direct verified-business checks and RFQ reads require login; contact helper retains Paid/Broker/Admin. Document these inconsistencies; a UI switch is not a blanket policy override.
